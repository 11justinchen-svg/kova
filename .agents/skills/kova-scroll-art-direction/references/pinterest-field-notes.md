# Pinterest field notes

Research captured on 2026-07-16. Pinterest exposed public titles and metadata for these references; some full pin pages were login-gated or blocked automated opening. Treat this as a directional field guide, then visually verify live sources when a design task requires exact details.

## Closest reference clusters

Translate print alignment into persistent web axes.
- [Minimalistic editorial website layout](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/405886985189677109/) — Use restraint as a counterweight: one quiet spread after a dense fluorescent sequence makes the loud sections land harder.

### Kinetic typography

- [Love Type kinetic typography](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/154389093465628361/) — Treat letters as the primary moving material. Explore delayed assembly, cropping, replacement, and synchronized scale rather than adding motion around static headings.
- [Delayed square type tutorial](https://sk.pinterest.com/pin/305400418503252278/) — Borrow the principle of delayed spatial propagation for a single hero lockup. Keep the delay short enough that the phrase reads as one event.
- [Abstract black-and-white kinetic typography poster](https://kr.pinterest.com/pin/graphic-design-poster-kinetic-typography--113927065556857844/) — Study contrast, repetition, and off-axis movement. Bring in color only where it sharpens the hierarchy.

### Swiss structure crossed with punk energy

- [Music posters explore Swiss modernism and punk rock](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/97601516900303014/) — Combine circles, strict geometry, limited color, and disciplined alignment with the urgency of music-poster scale. This is the model for “structured disruption.”
- [Vintage punk flyers redesigned in Swiss typography](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2674081016675792/) — Preserve punk compression and bluntness while cleaning the information hierarchy. Use rawness in crop and scale, not by making navigation confusing.
- [Swiss Punk theme](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/theme-swiss-punk--631559547779426776/) — Mix archival imagery, hard rules, condensed text, and irregular negative space. Avoid nostalgic pastiche; translate the logic into current interaction.

## Synthesized directions for Kova

### 1. Acid archive

Fluorescent mask, close photography, condensed vertical statement, issue numbers, and technical mono. Motion behaves like an archive sheet being scanned and indexed. This is the closest match to the supplied reference.

### 2. Swiss punk transmission

Black, paper white, and one signal color. Circles, rules, grid coordinates, and music-poster type collide with abrupt image swaps. Motion is mostly snapping, sliding, and hard cuts.

### 3. Plastic fashion specimen

Portrait or material photography sits beneath translucent sheets, irregular apertures, and registration marks. Motion peels, stretches, and aligns the layers while metadata names the specimen.

### 4. Kinetic type machine

Photography becomes secondary. One condensed phrase expands, replaces words, rotates, and locks to scroll states. Grid labels and a small progress rail keep the spectacle navigable.

### 5. Cyber-botanical terminal

A flower or organic specimen interrupts a near-black diagnostic grid. Signal-red CJK type and cold cyan image separation frame the subject. Scanners and data rails reveal real content; decorative glitches remain brief.

### 6. Photocopied field journal

Warm paper, coarse black type, modular landscape fragments, sparse star marks, and marginal observations create a humane eco-editorial voice. Motion assembles the image tiles and then becomes quiet.

### 7. Retro travel monument

A single landmark breaks through monumental condensed type on cream stock. Dusty cyan and oxidized red create archival warmth. Scroll reinforces the landmark's vertical rise while body copy remains calm.


## Research rule

Extract mechanisms—palette ratio, crop behavior, type hierarchy, mask logic, motion timing, and information placement. Do not reproduce a pin’s full composition, artwork, logo, or proprietary imagery. Record source links for any reference that materially influences a design proposal.
